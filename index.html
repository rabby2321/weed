// arc raiders verdict

Arc Raiders PvP punishes impairment harder than most shooters — the short TTK means whoever shoots first wins, third-person peeks require precise timing, and you're constantly juggling ARC threats and enemy raiders at once. Weed tanks all of those. The one argument for it is staying calm when your expensive loadout is on the line — but that calm won't save you when a sober squad one-taps you from a shoulder peek before you even process they're there. Play sober if you care about your gear.

* individual results vary by tolerance & dose. some habitual users report no subjective impairment — measurable reaction time deficits still occur regardless.
