// conclusion

Weed objectively hurts your competitive play — slower reactions, worse aim, and less game sense are all measurable and consistent. The only real upside is feeling less tilted and having more fun, but feeling like you're playing well and actually playing well are two very different things. If rank matters, play sober. If you just want to vibe, that's on you — but don't expect to frag out.

* effects vary by tolerance, dose & individual. heavy users may feel unimpaired — measurable deficits still occur.
